Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026. Everything stays on your iPhone.

The short version

Everything stays on your iPhone. We don't collect anything, we can't see anything, and we don't have a server.

How Glowjar uses Apple's Screen Time

Glowjar is built on Apple's Screen Time. When you choose apps, you pick them on Apple's own list, and Glowjar only receives sealed tokens from Apple. It can't read the names of the apps you picked, and it can't see your browsing or what you do inside any app. Apple tells Glowjar only when you pass a usage step you set up, such as "15 minutes used", so the jar can drain and your apps can fall asleep.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • Your daily limit, weekend limit, bedtime and focus nap times
  • How much of today's jar is used, and a log of past days (for your skies, streak and insights)
  • How long you said you used these apps before, to estimate the time you got back
  • Your "why", if you write one
  • Your Promise dates, your color mood and jar character, and whether you have Pro

This information is stored only on your device. Your device's own iCloud or computer backups may include it; those backups are controlled by you and by Apple.

What we collect

Nothing. Glowjar contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools. We don't sell or share your data, because we never receive it.

Purchases

Glowjar Pro is sold through Apple. Apple handles your payment and tells the app whether you have Pro. We never see your card details. Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Glowjar shows reminders such as "Half your glow is left" and "5 minutes left". They are created on your iPhone; nothing is sent through our servers. You can turn them off in iPhone Settings.

Sharing

Nothing leaves the app unless you choose to share it. If you share a sky poster, it goes wherever you send it, and that app's own privacy rules then apply.

Children

Glowjar isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
