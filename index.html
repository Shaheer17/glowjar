Glowjar

Pour your day into a jar. When it's empty, your apps fall asleep until midnight.

What it is

Glowjar is a friendly daily limit for the apps that take more of your time than you meant to give them. Pour your minutes into the jar; when it's empty, the apps you chose fall asleep until midnight. Calls, messages and everything else keep working.

Getting started

  • Open Glowjar and allow Screen Time access in Apple's box.
  • Pick the apps or categories to guard on Apple's list.
  • Pour your daily minutes and tap "Fill my jar".

Common questions

The jar moves in steps. Apple's Screen Time reports usage in steps of about 5 minutes, so the jar shows a range, such as "between 25 and 30 minutes left".

I can't raise my limit. Bigger jars arrive at midnight. During a Promise, the limit can't go up until the Promise ends.

How do I turn Glowjar off? Settings → Turn off Glowjar, any time your apps aren't asleep. During a Promise, the only way out is iPhone Settings → Screen Time, then turning off access for Glowjar.

I can't delete apps. A running Promise blocks deleting apps from the iPhone, so Glowjar can't be deleted either. iOS can't block deleting just one app. It ends when the Promise ends.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone. Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Cancel a subscription. iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Glowjar Pro.
